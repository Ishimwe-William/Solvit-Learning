## Video 1: Why You Feel Lost in Life?

How can you serve the world?

#1 Imagine a TED conference where the speaker inspires everyone, including yourself. The speaker is you, 20 years in the future. What are you talking about, and what’s so inspiring about it?

#2 Imagine a world where you have all the money and all the time. How would you use your talents and skills to serve others?

#3 Visualise the eulogy at your funeral, where your friends and family are saying nice things about you. But what would people positively impacted by your work say?

Reduce the effect of fear.

#1 What would you like to do if you knew you could not fail at it?
a. In terms of health, work & relationships.
b. What would you like to do, learn, try, or experiment with?
c. What impact would you like to have?

#2 What would you do even if you knew you would fail at it?

#3 What’s one dream you’ve always had but never pursued?
a. Why is it a dream for you?
b. What is stopping you from pursuing it?

Envision your dream life?

#1 What does your ideal Tuesday (week) look like? (a year from now)
a. What are you doing? Who are you with?
b. How are you spending your time?
c. What makes this day perfect for you?

#2 Imagine you’ve achieved everything you’ve ever wanted. How have you changed as a person? What qualities and achievements define this future version of yourself?

Use other people

#1 Who are the people you most admire and why? What quality do they possess that you wish to emulate?

Begin with the end in mind.

#1 What would it look like if you wrote your own obituary? (think relationship & work/achievements)

## Video 2: How to Figure Out What You Really Want in Life?

Where we are right now

#1 When it comes to work right now, what is working and what is not working?

#2 When it comes to my life right now, what is working and what’s not?

Within each category, split it into 3 more components (totalling 10):
i. Work: Growth, money & mission
ii. Health: Body, mind & soul
iii. Relationships: Romance, family & friends
iv. Extra: Joy

#3 Rate yourself from 1-10, how aligned do you feel in this area of your life?
a. How satisfied am I with the progress I’m making in this area of my life?

The truth exercise

#4 What are 10 things that are true about you? (positive statements only)

How to actually know what you want

#1 Think about a time in your life when you did something well and it brought you a sense of fulfilment. Find 3 or 5 moments. It has to include:
a. A concrete action (You were the main protagonist)
b. You believe you did well
c. It brought you a sense of fulfilment

#2 Look back on your calendar or the activities you’ve done in the last two weeks and identify which ones gave you energy and which ones drained your energy. (Use ++ and  - -)

#3 What are some 10 truths that you’d like to be true about yourself 12 months from now? ( Write it in the present verb)


## Video 3: How to Change Your Life in a Year 

The Dreamlining Method
In 10 years,
1. What do you want to learn?
2. Who do you want to be?
3. What do you want to see?
4. Where do you want to go?
5. What do you want to contribute to?
6. What do you want to have?
7. What do you want to try?
8. What do you want to do?
9. What do you want to create?
10. What do you want to overcome?

Keep the pen going. Go for quantity, not quality. Don’t think too much about it. If you put it down on the list, it will happen. Don’t limit yourself. Whatever you want.

The Odyssey Plan
#1 Write out, in detail, what your life would look like 5 years from now if you continued down your current path.

#2 Write out, in detail, what your life would look like 5 years from now if you took a completely different path.

#3 Write out, in detail, what your life would look like 5 years from now if money and social obligations, and what people would think, were completely irrelevant.

The Month 12 Celebration
Pick 3-5 goals you are going to focus on for the next 12 months. 
#1 Why is this goal important to you?
#2 How are you going to do it?
#3 When do you plan on working on it?
