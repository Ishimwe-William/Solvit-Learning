🎧 WEEK 3: COMMUNICATION (WITH OTHERS)
Inspired by "Listening Lab" & "Feedback That Builds" sessions

 Weekly Intention
This week, we shift from expressing ourselves and to truly hearing others. You'll practice active listening and giving feedback that builds people up.

Big Question: "How can listening make me a better communicator—and a better person?"

 📖 Before Session 1
Watch (required):
- Video: "The Power of Active Listening" : https://youtu.be/qpnNsSyDw-g?si=dRnfQLYZ0xaBJQB6
